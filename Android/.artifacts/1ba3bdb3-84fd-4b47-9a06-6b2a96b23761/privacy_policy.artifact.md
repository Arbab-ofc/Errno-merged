## A. Introduction

Errno operates the ERRNO mobile application and platform ("Platform"), which connects shopkeepers and business owners ("Shopkeepers") with workers and independent service providers ("Workers") for on-demand workforce needs, shift booking, and task management.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you download, install, or use the ERRNO mobile application, website, or related services. By using ERRNO, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Platform.

---

## B. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our services, facilitate connections between Shopkeepers and Workers, process payments, and ensure platform security.

### 1. Personal Information
- **Full Name:** To identify users and personalize profiles.
- **Mobile Number:** Primary identifier used for authentication, OTP verification, and direct communication between matched users.
- **Email Address:** Used for account communications, support, and receipts (`support.errno@gmail.com`).
- **Profile Information:** User type, professional skills, or business details.

### 2. Account Information
- **Login Credentials:** Phone number and OTP verification tokens.
- **Role Selection:** Whether registered as a Shopkeeper or Worker.
- **Account Preferences & Status:** Verification status, active shifts, and operational history.

### 3. Location Information
- **Precise & Approximate Location:** We collect GPS and network-based location data when you use the app to browse nearby jobs, display store locations for workers, or calculate distances between workers and job sites.
- **Usage Control:** Location access is requested via standard Android system permissions. You can control or revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings, though certain location-dependent features (like nearby job matching and map navigation) may be limited.

### 4. Transaction Information
- **Booking & Service Records:** Job titles, work categories, shift durations, rates, and acceptance timestamps.
- **Payment Status & Transaction Records:** UPI transaction IDs, amounts, and settlement records.
- **Payment Credentials Note:** ERRNO does not store debit/credit card numbers or sensitive banking credentials directly; these are handled securely by certified third-party payment gateway partners.

### 5. Device & Technical Information
- **IP Address & Device Identifiers:** Device model, Android version, and unique device identifiers.
- **App Diagnostics:** Crash logs, performance metrics, and error reports to ensure app stability.

### 6. Communication & Support Information
- **Customer Support Records:** Messages, support tickets, and call logs with our customer service team (`+91 9971439107`).
- **Ratings & Reviews:** Feedback and ratings given between Shopkeepers and Workers upon job completion.
